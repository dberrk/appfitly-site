Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026 · Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Fitly collects, uses and shares consumer health data. It is written for people in the United States, including residents of Washington (My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373), Nevada (NRS chapter 603A, as amended by Senate Bill 370 of 2023) and Connecticut (the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, as amended for consumer health data). Our general Privacy Policy covers everything else.

Who we areFitly, an AI fitness and nutrition coaching app, operated by Berk Demir (sole proprietor), Nevşehir / Merkez, Türkiye
Contactdestek@appfitly.com

1 Consumer health data we collect and how we use it

"Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status. In Fitly, that is:

We use consumer health data only to provide the Fitly features you use. We will not collect a new category of consumer health data, or use it for a new purpose, without first telling you and getting your consent.

2 Where the data comes from

  • From you: what you enter during onboarding, log in the app, write to Fitly Coach, answer in check-ins, or capture in a meal photo.
  • From your device: Apple Health and Apple Watch, only for the data types you allow in iOS.
  • Derived by Fitly: targets, plan adjustments, reviews and coach notes calculated from the data above, on your device, on our servers or by our AI providers.

4 We do not sell consumer health data

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not exchange it for money or anything else of value.

5 Your consent and how to withdraw it

We ask for consent before we collect or share consumer health data. During onboarding, before anything is stored or sent, the app shows two separate consents. Both are off until you turn them on:

  1. Health data consent: lets Fitly collect and use your body, workout, nutrition and recovery data for coaching.
  2. AI transfer consent: lets Fitly send your coach questions and context to the AI model providers listed in Section 3.

Apple Health access is asked for separately by iOS, and you choose each data type there.

How to withdraw:

  • Fitly consents: in the app, Settings → Consent management. The features that need that consent stop working, and no new data is collected or sent under it. Withdrawing does not delete data you already gave us; to delete it, see Section 6.
  • Apple Health: iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Fitly.
  • Or e-mail us at destek@appfitly.com.

6 Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:

  • Confirm and access: learn whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it. In the app: Settings → "Download your data".
  • Get a list of recipients: the third parties and affiliates we have shared your consumer health data with, with an e-mail address or online way to contact each of them.
  • Withdraw consent: see Section 5.
  • Delete: in the app, Settings → "Delete account". This deletes your account data from our database. We also tell our processors and any recipient of your data to delete it. Copies in backups are removed when the backup retention period ends, and in any case within six months.
  • Review and correct: you can see and change your profile, weight entries, workout and meal logs in the app, and see or delete what Fitly Coach has learned about you in Settings → "How Fitly Coach knows you". You can also ask us by e-mail to correct your data.

How to make a request. Use the in-app options above, or e-mail destek@appfitly.com with the subject "Consumer health data request" and say which right you want to use. You do not need to create an account. To protect your data we need to confirm the request comes from you: we may ask you to write from the e-mail address on your Fitly account, or to send the request from inside the app. If you use Fitly without an e-mail address, the in-app options are the most reliable way, because we cannot otherwise link an e-mail to your account.

Response time and cost. We answer within 45 days of receiving your request. If we need more time, we may extend this once by another 45 days, and we will tell you why within the first 45 days. Requests are free up to twice in any 12 months.

Appeal. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, or by e-mailing destek@appfitly.com with the subject "Consumer health data appeal". We will answer in writing within 45 days and explain what we did and why. If we deny your appeal, you can file a complaint with your state's Attorney General:

  • Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint
  • Nevada: ag.nv.gov/Complaints
  • Connecticut: dir.ct.gov/ag/complaint

We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

7 How we process and protect the data

  • Account data is stored with Supabase in the European Union (Ireland), sent over encrypted connections, and separated per account by database access rules, so one user cannot read another user's data.
  • Apple Health data (other than imported weights), meal photo thumbnails and some screen caches stay on your device and are removed when you sign out or delete the app.
  • We keep consumer health data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account.
  • Consumer health data is never written to our crash reports or product analytics.

8 Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will tell you in the app or by e-mail before it takes effect, and update the date at the top of this page. If the change means collecting a new category of consumer health data, using it for a new purpose, or sharing it with a new category of recipient, we will ask for your consent first.
