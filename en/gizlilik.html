4 Special-Category Health Data and Explicit Consent

We process your health data solely on the basis of your explicit consent. During onboarding, before your data is first stored or transmitted, two separate consents are presented (both off by default):

  1. Health data processing consent — processing your body/exercise/nutrition data for coaching.
  2. International transfer consent — transferring chat and AI context to model providers abroad (see Section 5).

If you do not grant either consent, the related feature will not work and the data you entered up to that point is discarded without being stored. You can withdraw your explicit consent at any time via Settings → Consent management; withdrawal takes effect prospectively and does not delete past records (for deletion, see Section 8).

7 Apple HealthKit

Data accessed via HealthKit (e.g. weight) is processed only on your device; in accordance with Apple Guideline 5.1.3, it is never used for advertising, never sold to third parties, never transferred anywhere outside iCloud and is not sent to AI providers in raw form.

8 Retention and Destruction (Art. 7)

  • Your personal data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), all rows belonging to you are deleted from the database in a cascade.
  • Account deletion also removes your RevenueCat customer record and subscription data through the existing in-service deletion flow.
  • The Apple attribution response and UUID link are deleted from Supabase. The one-way SHA-256 replay hash expires after 24 hours and is removed by the hourly purge within 25 hours. A separate abuse/security record holds the UUID, counter and timestamps plus a lease hash in a fixed 25-hour window, holds an active lease for 45 seconds, and cascades on account deletion.
  • The non-personal shared recipe cache is not tied to any individual and is therefore outside the scope of deletion.
  • The temporary export file created for your data download request automatically becomes inaccessible after 48 hours.
  • Deletion in backups is completed within Supabase's backup retention window.

9 Your Rights (KVKK Art. 11)

Under KVKK Art. 11, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is being processed, to request information, to learn the purpose of processing, to request its correction/deletion, to learn the third parties to whom it has been transferred, and to object to processing. Within the App:

  • Download your portable account data — Settings → "Download your data": your portable account data, including the Apple attribution response, is sent via a secure link. Short-lived replay, security, and budget ledgers are excluded to preserve security integrity.
  • Delete your account and account-linked data — Settings → "Delete account".
  • View/delete the Council's inferences about you — Settings → "How the Council knows you".
  • Withdraw your consent — Settings → Consent management.

You may submit your requests to destek@appfitly.com; your requests are resolved within the period prescribed by KVKK (at most 30 days).

10 Cookies and Tracking

Fitly is a mobile app; it does not perform advertising tracking (cross-app tracking) and does not require Apple ATT permission. StoreKit version equality is only a first-acquisition-version candidate that excludes upgrades; it cannot conclusively exclude a same-version redownload or an acquisition on another device. After authentication, iOS sends the AdServices token to our Edge Function, which posts it transiently to Apple, never stores the raw token, and stores Apple's standard response in Supabase in the EU. Apple's conversionType is authoritative for Download/Redownload/PreOrder. A local owner record blocks ordinary account changes only while that installation remains, and expected-owner/JWT equality prevents an in-flight A→B race. We do not collect IDFA or track you across apps. RevenueCat Apple Search Ads and RevenueCat→PostHog integrations are disabled. PostHog receives only bounded client funnel events when your analytics choice permits; automatic screen capture and session recording are off, and analytics can be disabled in Settings.
