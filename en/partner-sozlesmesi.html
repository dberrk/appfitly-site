Fitly Partner Programme Agreement

Version 1.0 · Last updated: 29 July 2026 · In force from: 29 July 2026

This English text is provided for convenience. The Turkish version is the binding one; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

1 Parties and Acceptance

This Partner Programme Agreement ("Agreement") is between Berk Demir, based in Nevşehir, Türkiye ("Fitly", "we") and the individual or entity joining the Fitly Partner Programme ("Partner", "you"). Signing in to the partner panel, sharing the code assigned to you, or claiming commission under the programme means you accept this Agreement.

This Agreement applies together with the Fitly Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Where they conflict on matters concerning the programme relationship, this Agreement prevails.

2 Status: Independent Party

The Partner is an independent party. This Agreement creates no employment, partnership, joint venture, agency, distributorship or representation relationship. The Partner has no authority to make statements, give undertakings, sign contracts or otherwise bind Fitly.

The Partner is solely responsible for their own taxes, social security and regulatory obligations, and for their own equipment, team and costs. Fitly reimburses none of these.

3 The Partner Code

Fitly assigns the Partner one or more discount codes. Codes remain Fitly's property; the Partner receives only the right to use them within the programme, for the term of this Agreement.

  • Codes are not transferable and may not be sold or sub-licensed.
  • The Partner may not use their own code for their own purchase or on behalf of their own household. Doing so forfeits the commission.
  • Fitly may cap redemptions, set an expiry, and disable or change a code at any time.
  • The Partner must notify Fitly immediately if a code is misused or obtained by third parties.

4 Commission

The Partner earns commission on paid subscription sales made by users who entered the Partner's code in the app. The commission rate is recorded in the partner panel, and the rate shown there governs.

Commission is calculated on the net revenue of a sale. Net revenue is the amount actually remaining to Fitly after the app store's commission and applicable taxes are deducted from what the user paid. It is not calculated on the gross amount the user paid.

Fitly may change the rate. A rate change applies only going forward; commission on sales made before the change is never recalculated.

Commission arises only on genuine, paid transactions. Trials, free periods, sandbox or test transactions, promotional grants and complimentary access do not earn commission.

5 Maturity and Payment

Commission on a sale becomes payable 45 days after the end of the calendar month in which the sale was made. This matches the app store's refund window and revenue remittance schedule; Fitly is not obliged to pay commission on revenue it has not yet received.

Payments are recorded against the relevant month in the partner panel. An amount shown as "Paid" is a payment that has been made for that month.

Documentation and tax

Where the Partner is required by the law applying to them to issue an invoice or equivalent document, payment is made after that document reaches Fitly.

Where the law requires an amount to be withheld at source, payment is made net of that amount and the withheld sum is remitted to the relevant authority on the Partner's behalf.

The Partner's own income tax, VAT and social security obligations are theirs (see section 2). Fitly is not obliged to file or pay on the Partner's behalf.

The payment method, currency and any minimum payment amount are agreed by the parties in writing. A balance below the agreed minimum is not forfeited; it carries over and is paid once the threshold is reached.

6 Refunds, Cancellations and Clawback

If a subscription is refunded, cancelled or reversed by chargeback, commission on that sale is treated as never having arisen.

  • If not yet paid, it is deducted from the balance owed.
  • If already paid, it is offset against later earnings. If there are no later earnings to offset, the Partner refunds the amount.
  • Commission found to arise from fraud, fabricated transactions or breach of this Agreement is clawed back even if already paid.

7 Nature of the Figures in the Panel

Figures in the partner panel are estimates. The app store reports commission and tax rates per transaction and with a delay. The definitive figure is established by the store's monthly revenue report to Fitly, and that report governs in the event of a dispute.

For privacy, the panel shows aggregate counts only. The identity, e-mail address, purchase timing or health data of the people who use a code is never shared with the Partner under any circumstances.

8 Disclosure Obligation

The Partner must disclose the material connection with Fitly in every piece of content where the code is shared, in a way an ordinary viewer will readily notice.

  • In Türkiye: a clear label such as #reklam or #işbirliği, as required by the Ministry of Trade's Regulation on Commercial Advertising and Unfair Commercial Practices and its Guide on Social Media Influencers.
  • For content directed at the United States: a clear and conspicuous disclosure under FTC 16 CFR Part 255 (Endorsement Guides).
  • The disclosure appears at the start of the content, is not hidden behind a "show more" control, and is in the same language as the content.

Fines and claims arising from a breach of this obligation are the Partner's responsibility. Fitly may require content without a disclosure to be corrected or removed.

9 Prohibited Conduct

The Partner undertakes not to:

  • use their own code for their own purchase, or arrange accounts for that purpose;
  • create fake accounts, bots, automated installs or incentivised fake sign-ups;
  • distribute the code through unsolicited bulk messages, comment spam or misleading notifications;
  • run brand keyword advertising on "Fitly", "appfitly" or similar terms, or create accounts, domains or apps that appear to be an official Fitly channel;
  • publish the code on coupon or discount aggregator sites without permission;
  • make false statements about Fitly's prices, features, discount levels or results;
  • promote Fitly alongside unlawful, hateful or obscene content, or content that infringes the rights of others.

10 Health Claims

⚠ The most important rule in this programme

Fitly is not a medical device or a healthcare service. When promoting Fitly, the Partner must not claim treatment, diagnosis, cure of any condition, or a guaranteed outcome.

In particular, statements such as "makes you lose X kilos", "cures this condition", "replaces medication" or "guaranteed results", and fabricated before and after imagery, are prohibited. If personal experience is described, it must be stated that it is personal experience and will not produce the same result for everyone.

11 Use of the Brand

Fitly grants the Partner a non-exclusive, non-transferable and freely revocable licence, for the term of this Agreement only, to use the Fitly name, logo and promotional assets within the programme.

Assets may not be altered or used in a way that could be confused with another brand. The licence ends automatically when this Agreement ends, and the Partner removes such use from existing content within a reasonable period.

12 Content and Intellectual Property

The Partner owns the promotional content they create. The Partner grants Fitly a free, worldwide, transferable right to re-share that content on Fitly's own channels. Fitly credits the Partner when sharing, and removes the content within a reasonable period on request.

13 Personal Data

Within the programme Fitly processes the Partner's name, contact e-mail, panel account and code performance records. The legal basis is the formation and performance of this Agreement (Turkish DP Law art. 5/2-c; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) where applicable). Data is hosted on Supabase infrastructure in the European Union (Ireland).

Fitly holds no bank, IBAN or tax identification details for the Partner; payment details are handled outside the panel, under section 5. The Partner's data subject rights are exercised through the route described in the Privacy Policy.

The Partner keeps confidential any commercial information learned through the programme, such as unannounced features, pricing plans and sales figures.

14 Suspension, Termination and Leaving

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time. The Partner submits a leave request from the panel; once accepted, their codes close to new redemptions.

Leaving does not forfeit commission already earned. Commission on sales made before termination and not refunded is paid on the normal maturity schedule in section 5.

Fitly may suspend a Partner without notice in case of breach, suspected fraud, damage to the brand or unlawful use. Where fraud or breach is established, the related commission is clawed back under section 6.

Sections 6, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16 and 18 survive termination.

15 Limitation of Liability

Fitly gives no guarantee of earnings, sales volume, reach or continuity under the programme. The programme is not a promise of income.

Fitly's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total commission actually paid to the Partner in the twelve months before the claim arose. Fitly is not liable for indirect loss, loss of profit, loss of reputation or loss of data.

Fitly is not responsible for changes to the app store's pricing, commission, refund or reporting policies.

16 Indemnity

The Partner agrees to indemnify Fitly against claims, proceedings, administrative fines and reasonable legal costs brought against Fitly as a result of the Partner breaching this Agreement, failing to disclose, making false or health related claims, or infringing the rights of third parties.

17 Assignment, Changes and Severability

The Partner may not assign this Agreement without Fitly's written consent. Fitly may assign it as part of a transfer of the business.

Fitly may update this Agreement. Material changes are notified to the e-mail address on the Partner's panel account and take effect 15 days after notice. The Partner may leave within that period; commission earned is then paid under section 14.

If any provision is held invalid, the remainder stays in force.
